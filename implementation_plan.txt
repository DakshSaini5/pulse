## Deployment Strategy & Timeline

### Stage 1: Full Testing & QA (Estimated: 1-2 Days)
- Fix all CSS and routing bugs.
- Perform End-to-End (E2E) testing on the Android Emulator and Web Browser.
- Verify Google OAuth works flawlessly in production mode.

### Stage 2: Web Deployment (Estimated: 1 Day)
- **Frontend Hosting:** Deploy the React/Vite app to **Vercel** for lightning-fast global CDN delivery.
- **Backend Hosting:** Deploy the Node.js/Express API to **Render** or **Railway**.
- **Database:** Keep using the existing Supabase PostgreSQL instance.
- **Action:** Point your custom domain (e.g., `pulse.com`) to Vercel.

### Stage 3: Play Store Launch (Estimated: 3-5 Days)
- **Day 1:** Generate the signed production release App Bundle (`.aab`) instead of a `.apk`.
- **Day 2:** Fill out the Google Play Store listing (App Icon, Screenshots, Description, Privacy Policy URL).
- **Day 3-5:** Submit for Google Play Review. Google typically takes 2-4 days to review new healthcare-related apps.

**Tentative Launch Date:** If we finish Phase 1-3 today, the website can be live **tomorrow**, and the Android app will be live on the Play Store in approximately **1 week** (pending Google's review process).
